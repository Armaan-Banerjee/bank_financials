percentile in the distribution Is estabtished that most closely’
corresponded to the scenario. Probability weight for base case is set

Use of the scenarios in Commercial lending

The Commercial lending ECL methodology is based on the concept of
CCls. The CCls raprasent, similar to the exogenous component in
Retail, all relevant economic loss drivers for a region/industry segment
aggregated into a single index value that describes the loss rate
conditions in the respective segment relative to its long-run average. A
CCI value of zero corresponds to loss rates at long-run average levels,
a positive CC! value corresponds to loss rates below long-run average
levels and a negative CC! value corresponds to loss rates above long-
run average levels.

The individual economic scenarios are translated into forward-looking
prajections of CCis using a set of econometric modals. Subsequently
the CCI projections for the individual scenarios are averaged into a
single central CCI projection according to the given scenario
probabilities. The central CCI projection is then overiaid with an
additional mean reversion assumption to gradually revert to the tong-
run average CCI value of zero in the outer years of the projection
horizon.

Finally, ECL Is calculated using a Monte Carlo approach by averaging
PD and LGD values arising from many CC] paths simulated around
the central CCI projection.

The rationale for the Commercial approach is the long-standing
observation that loss rates in Commercial portfolios tend to faltow
regular cycles. This allows Coutts to enrich the range and depth of
future economic conditions embedded In the final ECL beyond what
would be obtained from using the discrete macro-economic scenarios -
alone,

Interest rate risk

Non-traded interest rate risk (NTIRR) arises from the provision to
cllents of a range of banking products with differing interest rate
characteristics. When aggregated, these products form portfolios of
assets and liabilities with varying degrees of sensitivity to changes in
market interest rates, Mismatches can give rise to volatility in net
interest income as interest rates vary.

NTIRR comprises the following three primary risk types:

« Gap risk ~ arises from the timing of rate changes in non-trading
“book instruments. Tha extant of gap risk depends on whether
‘ changes to the term structure of interest rates accur consistently
across the yield curve (parallel risk) or differentially by period
(non-parailel risk).

.* Basis risk ~ captures the Impact of relative changes in interest
rates for financial instruments that have similar tenors but are -
priced using different interest rate Indices, or on the same interest
rate indices’ but with different tenors.

+ Option risk ~ arises from option derivative positions or fram
optional alements embedded in assets, fiabilities and/or off-
balance shaet items, where Coutts or its client can alter tha level
and timing of their cash flows.

NTIRR can be measured from either an economic value-based or
eamings-based perspective, or a combination of the two. Value-
based approaches measure the change in value of the balance sheet
assets and liabilities including all cash flows. Eamings-based
approaches measure the potential impact on the income statement of
changes in interest rates over a defined horizon, generally one to .
three years.

Coutts uses VaR as its value-based approach and sensitivity of net
Interest eamings as its eamings-based approach.

Value-at-risk

VaR is a statistical estimate of the potential change in the market
portfolio (and, thus the impact on the i income statement) over a
specified time horizon at a given confidence level.

Coutts has no foreign subsidiaries and manages its exposures to
overseas suppiters within specified limits. 1

rates, mainly because maturing structural hedges are replaced at
higher or lower rates and changes to coupons on managed rate client
products do not match changes in market rates of Interest or central
bank policy rates.

Eamings sensitivity is derived from a market-implied forward rate
curve. Assumptions are applied to thls curve to derive central bank

an Indication of underlying sensitivity to interest rate movements.

Reported sensitivities should not be considered a forecast of future
