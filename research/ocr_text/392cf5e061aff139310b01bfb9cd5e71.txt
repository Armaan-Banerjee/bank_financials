Summary of significant accounting policies

Going concern

The financial statements presented are based on a going concern basis as the company is satisfied that it
has adequate resources to continue operating for the foreseeable future. In making this assessment,
consideration was taken of the current and required level of resources in various scenarios.

Intangible assets

Oxbury FS Pic is in the process of building a cloud based technological system for the management of
future activities as the business matures through the defined steps in its business plan. This is a key element
to successfully achieving the business plan and generating a profitable revenue base.

The costs related to the build of the operating system consist of an external provider together with internal
effort. All the effort invalved is separately identifiable and the cost is measurable with a high degree of
confidence. The business plan currently relies upon the build of the operating system to generate future
revenues.

Taxation

Income tax on profits or losses for the year is either current or deferred. It is recognised to the extent that
it relates to items recognised directly in shareholders equity.

Any tax payable is based on taxable profit for the year, which may differ from accounting profit due to
either timing or absolute treatment. The taxation liability is based on the appropriate tax rates enacted
at that date.

Deferred tax is tax expected to be payable or recoverable on differences between the carrying amounts
of assets and liabilities in the financial statements and the tax bases used for taxable profit.

Deferred tax is calculated based on the relevant rate of tax which is expected to be enacted on
realisation of the asset or liability, and only offset when there is a legally enforceable right to do so.

Deferred Tax

The potential deferred tax asset has not been recognised for the current financial year as the
recoverobility is dependent on future taxable profits to offset against. The likelihood of those future
taxable profits is not considered to be sufficiently certain.

Share options

Executive employees have been allocated shares which vest based oan specific events. Each relevant
employee has paid for the shares at the neminal value, with all relevant monies having been received by
the company at the reporting date. The rights attaching to the shares result in the shares being deemed
to be valued at nominal value, and therefore have been purchased ot a price deemed to compare to
market value ot the grant date. As such, the shares have not been deemed to require an accounting entry
related to share-based payments.

Pensions
The company does not participate in a defined benefit pension scheme.

The company participates in a single defined contribution benefit scheme for employees and contributes
to defined contribution schemes for senior employees where the individual requests the company to do so.
The value of the contribution is based on a percentage of the basic salary of the individual and is recorded
as an expense, Unpaid but due contributions are recorded as a liability.
