REPORT OF THE DIRECTORS FOR THE PERIOD ENDED 31 MARCH 2018

The directors present their report and the financial statements of the Company for the period ended 31
March 2018.

Principal activities and business review

The Company was incorporated on 6 February 2017 with the name Biz Financial Services Limited. The
Company changed its name to Echo Financial Services Limited on 2 June 2017. The Company is in
the process of changing its name to Recognise Financial Services Limited.

In the second half of 2017, the directors explored the possibility of the Company applying for a UK
banking licence. On 31 January 2018, City of London Group plc acquired a 73% interest in the
Company and the Company commenced the process of applying for a UK banking licence in February
2018. This process is on-going.

The Company did not generate any revenue during the period to 31 March 2018. The loss for the
period comprises the costs incurred in preparing the licence application during the period.

Results and dividends
The loss for the period from 6 February 2017 to 31 March 2018 was £196,352. The directors do not
recommend payment of a dividend.

The directors have taken advantage of the small companies exemption provided by section 414B of the
Companies Act 2006 not to prepare a Strategic Report.

1 Company

The Company was incorporated on 6 February 2017 as a private limited company with the name Biz
Financial Services Limited and company number 10603119. The name of the Company was changed
to Echo Financial Services Limited on 2 June 2018. The Company is in the process of changing its
name to Recognise Financial Services Limited.

The Company's accounting reference date is 31 March. These financial statements are for the fourteen
month period from the date of incorporation, 6 February 2017, to 31 March 2018. As they are the first
statutory accounts of the Company, no comparative figures are shown.

The Company explored the possibility of applying for a UK banking licence in the second half of 2017
and commenced the process of preparing the application in February 2018 after City of London Group
plc acquired a 73% interest in the Company. The Company did not generate any income in the period
and its expenditure related wholly to its application for a UK banking licence.

2 Basis of preparation

The financial statements have been prepared in accordance with International Financial Reporting
Standards (IFRSs) as adopted by the European Union as they apply to the financial statements of the
Company for the period ended 31 March 2018 and applied in accordance with the Companies Act 2006.

The financial statements have been prepared on the historical cost basis.

Going concern

The financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis. The Company is in the process
of preparing a UK banking licence application for submission to the Prudential Regulation Authority.
Under the terms of a Shareholders’ Agreement, the parent company, City of London Group plc, was
committed as at 31 March 2018 to provide future funding of up to £1,500,000 to progress the UK banking
licence application by way of subscribing for deferred shares. If the Board of the Company determines
that additional funding is required, City of London Group ple and J S Oakley, a minority shareholder,
will provide Shareholder Loans in equal proportions up to an amount of £1,500,000 ie a maximum of
£750,000 each. The financial statements have therefore been prepared on a going concern basis.

3.1 Property, plant and equipment

Property, plant and equipment are stated at historical cost less accumulated depreciation. Depreciation
is provided to write off the cost less the estimated residual value of each asset by equal annual
instalments over their estimated useful economic lives as follows:

3.2 Corporation tax
Tax on the result for the period comprises current and deferred tax.

Current tax is the expected tax payable on the taxable income of the period, using tax rates enacted or
substantially enacted at the balance sheet date.

Deferred tax is provided using the balance sheet liability method, providing for temporary differences
between the carrying amounts of assets and liabilities for financial reporting purposes and the amounts
used for tax purposes. The amount of deferred tax provided is based on the expected manner of
realisation or settlement of the carrying amount of assets and liabilities, using tax rates enacted or
substantially enacted at the balance sheet date.

Deferred income tax assets are recognised to the extent that it is possible that future taxable profits will
be available against which the temporary differences can be utilised.

Deferred tax assets and liabilities are not discounted.

3.3 Judgements and estimates

The preparation of financial information in conformity with IFRS requires management to make
judgements, estimates and assumptions that affect the application of policies and reported amounts of
assets and liabilities, income and expenses. The estimates and associated assumptions are based on
historical experience and various other factors that are believed to be reasonable under the
circumstances, the results of which form the basis for making the judgements about carrying values of
assets and liabilities that are not readily apparent from other sources. Actual results may differ from
these estimates.

The estimates and underlying assumptions are reviewed on an on-going basis. Revisions to accounting
estimates are recognised in the period in which the estimate is revised if the revision affects only that
period or in the period of the revision and future periods if the revision affects both current and future
periods.

3.4 Risk Management
The objective of the Board is to set policies to reduce risk as far as possible whilst maintaining the
Company's ability to achieve its strategic objective.

In connection with the preparation of its application for a UK banking licence, the Company has
developed detailed plans on how it would intend to develop and manage its business activities should
its application for a UK banking licence be successful. As part of this exercise, it has considered the
risks that could affect the business and the steps that the Company would take to mitigate these risks,
which include the following:

legal and regulatory risk

capital management

liquidity risk

credit risk

interest rate risk

business continuity

cyber risk

people/ succession risk

reputational risk

The main risks for the Company during the period prior to a decision being given by the Prudential
Regulation Authority on the Company's application for a UK banking licence, centre on the perceived
strength of its application and the liquidity risk that it has sufficient cash reserves to enable it to meet its
financial liabilities as they fall due during this period.

In order to optimise the strength of its application, the Company has recruited a team of people with
extensive relevant UK banking experience, including the establishment of a new banking operation, and
has appointed external advisors with relevant experience.

The Company has mitigated the liquidity risk by initially preparing detailed budgets which include cash
flows projecting several years into the future. On the basis of these budgets, the Company has secured
funding for the medium term through the Shareholders’ Agreement under which its shareholders have
undertaken to provide further funding of up to £3,000,000 (see note 17).
