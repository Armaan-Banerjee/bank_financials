(a) Basis of consolidation and accounting date

(i) The financial statements of the group are made up for the 52 weeks to 13th January,
1990. Since the group accounting date is virtually co-terminous with the calendar
year 1989 the financial year’s figures are headed 1989 and the corresponding figures
for the previous year are headed 1988. The financial statements have been prepared
under the historical cost convcntien and in accordance with Sections 258 and
259 of the Companies Act 1985 and 8~hedule 9 to that Act.

(ii) Associated companies
In the consolidated balance sheet associated companies are shown at cost, including
advances, and the group's share of rescrves from the date of acquisition, less
amounts written off.

(iii) Premium on acquisition of shares
The premium paid on the acquisition of shares in subsidiaries, being the excess
of the amount paid over the net tangible assets at the date of acquisition, has been
written off against reserves.

(b) Investments

(i) Investments are valued at cost adjusted for the amortization of premiums or
discounts in the purchase price of dated stock,

(ii) Income from investments includes amortization of premiums and discounts on
dated stock, which {s taken in equal annual instalments from the date of acquisition
to maturity. .

(iii) Realised profits and losses on sales of investments are taken to the profit and loss
account in the year in which they arise,

(c) Losses on advances
The amount charged against operating profit for losses on advances comprises specific
provision against identifiable losses and a general provision to cover latent but
unidentifiable losses due to doubtful debts, Both provisions are based on a year end
appraisal of advances. Advances are shown on the balance sheet after deducting those
provisions.

{d) Depreciation
Depreciution is provided on a straight line basis at the following rates which are «
estimated to write down the assets to realisable values at the end of their useful lives:

Frechold and long leaschold buildings 2.5 per cent per annum

Short leasehold buildings life of lease

Equipment 10 to 20 per cent per
annum

Vehicles 25 per cent per annum

(ec) Leasing

Assets leased to customers are valued at original cost less depreciation, which is
calculated to write off tliat cost over the primary period of the lease. Depreciation for
the period represents the full amount of leasing payments due in the period, less the
amounts credited to the profit and loss account.

Income from assets leased to customers is credited to the profit and loss account in
proportion to the funds invested and includes amounts in respect of both regional
development grants and tax credits which arise from falling corporation tax rates, grossed
up at the average rate of corporation tax applicable to the period.
