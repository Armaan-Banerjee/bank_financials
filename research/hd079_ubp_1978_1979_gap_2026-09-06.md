# HD-079 UBP UK: FY1978–FY1979 apparent filing gap (2026-09-06)

## Conclusion

The gap is real in the *itemised online Companies House filing history*, but it
does not establish that Hambros Bank failed to prepare accounts.  The official
record moves from an accounts filing for the year ended 31 March 1977 to an
accounts filing for the year ended 31 March 1980.  In between, its only visible
entry is a 19 April 1979 officer appointment.  There is no separate official
online `AA` accounts row for a period ended in 1978 or 1979.

The 1980 accounts nevertheless publish a **FY1979 comparative**: their profit
and loss account and balance sheet each have 1980 and 1979 columns.  Therefore
FY1979 is available from a primary statutory-account source, albeit as a
comparative rather than a separately catalogued filing.  FY1978 is not present
in either the FY1977 or FY1980 report and cannot safely be reconstructed from
the documents currently available.

The likeliest explanation is an archive/cataloguing discontinuity rather than
a nonstandard accounting period: Companies House explicitly records a
non-online `PRE87` item, “A selection of documents registered before 1 January
1987,” which can be requested for a fee.  The record does not enumerate that
bundle’s contents, so it is not evidence that it contains the two missing
accounts.  It is the appropriate next primary-source route for FY1978 and,
if a standalone report is required, FY1979.

## Evidence

Union Bancaire Privee (UK) Limited is the same company, **00964058**, that was
named Hambros Bank Limited from incorporation on 16 October 1969 until 2
November 1998.  The gap is therefore not explained by an entity change.
[Companies House overview](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/00964058)

The official filing-history page containing the oldest account rows records:

| Filed date | Companies House type and description | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| 26 August 1980 | `AA` — Accounts made up to 31 March 1980 (18 pages) | Online statutory source exists. |
| 19 April 1979 | `288a` — New secretary appointed | This is the immediately preceding displayed event. |
| 6 September 1977 | `AA` — Accounts made up to 31 March 1977 (21 pages) | Online statutory source exists. |
| 1 January 1987 | `PRE87` — A selection of documents registered before 1 January 1987 | Not online; Companies House offers “Request Document.” |

Source: [official Companies House filing history, page 29](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/00964058/filing-history?page=29) and [page 30](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/00964058/filing-history?page=30), accessed 6 September 2026.  The ordered entries show no `AA` account item for a 1978 or 1979 period.  Page 30 also confirms the normal 31 March 1977 year end, and the FY1980 filing confirms the next visible accounts period remains 31 March; there is no primary-record evidence here of an unusual 1978/79 accounting-reference-date change.

The supplied [FY1980 statutory accounts](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/00964058/filing-history/MjAyMzcwNTAwOGFkaXF6a2N4/document?format=pdf&download=0) are headed “Statement of Accounts of Hambros Bank Limited for the year ended 31st March, 1980.”  Its profit and loss account and balance sheet give 1980 and 1979 columns.  That makes the FY1979 figures primary-source comparative disclosures; it does **not** turn them into a separate filing or provide FY1978 figures.

## Implication for HD-079

- FY1979 can be transcribed from the FY1980 accounts, with a source note that it is the comparative column in the 31 March 1980 statutory accounts.
- Do not infer or interpolate FY1978.
- To resolve FY1978 conclusively, order/request the Companies House `PRE87` selection and inspect whether it contains the 31 March 1978 and/or 31 March 1979 accounts.  Until then, describe FY1978 as **not available in the itemised online register**, not as “not filed.”

## PRE95 bundle inspection (2026-09-06)

The two supplied pre-1995 downloads were byte-identical 122-page scans. Full
page OCR and visual review found incorporation, name-change, articles, and
capital-resolution material only (dated 1969–1989); neither contained annual
accounts, a balance sheet, a profit and loss account, or an accounting-period
report for FY1978 or FY1979. These bundles therefore do not resolve FY1978.
