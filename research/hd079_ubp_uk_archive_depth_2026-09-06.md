# HD-079 UBP UK archive-depth follow-up (2026-09-06)

## Result

The earlier HD-079 note materially understated the available archive. The
Companies House filing-history index for company **00964058** (Union Bancaire
Privée (UK) Limited and its predecessor names) exposes statutory accounts well
before FY2002:

| Period shown in filing history | Evidence |
|---|---|
| FY2012, FY2011, FY2010, FY2009, FY2008, FY2007, FY2006, FY2005, FY2004, FY2003, FY2002, FY2001, FY2000, FY1999, FY1998 | Full accounts listed for each period; the 1998 entry is explicitly group accounts for the short period to 28 February 1998 |
| FY1997, FY1996, FY1995, FY1994, FY1993, FY1992, FY1991, FY1990, FY1989, FY1988, FY1987, FY1986 | Full accounts listed, generally with 31 March year ends |
| FY1985, FY1984, FY1983, FY1982, FY1981, FY1980 | Annual accounts listed; FY1983 has an unusual 18 August year-end, so it needs period/basis review before treating it as a normal annual link |
| FY1977, FY1976, FY1975, FY1974, FY1973 | Annual accounts listed, generally 31 March year ends; the index has no FY1978/FY1979 account row |

The index therefore establishes a practical *filing-availability* floor of at
least FY1973, with a likely continuous run from FY1980 onward apart from the
FY1983 year-end change. It does **not** establish that every filing is
retrievable or suitable for the workbook: the underlying documents still need
to be obtained and checked for company-only versus group basis, units, period
end, accounting presentation, and restatements.

## Primary-source evidence

The official Companies House filing history confirms the current legal entity,
company number 00964058, and the name chain through Hambros Bank, SG Hambros
Bank & Trust, SG Hambros Bank, SG Kleinwort Hambros Bank, and UBP UK:
[Companies House overview](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/00964058)
and [official filing history](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/00964058/filing-history).

The indexed filing-history rendering exposes the older Companies House rows
that the official paginated page does not consistently return in this
environment. It lists FY2008–FY2007 and FY2006–FY2002 accounts, FY2001–FY1998
accounts, FY1997–FY1987 accounts, FY1986–FY1980 accounts, and FY1977–FY1973
annual accounts. See the dated rows in the [filing-history index](https://www.companiesintheuk.co.uk/company/union-bancaire-privee-uk/filing-history),
especially the entries for 2008–2002, 1997–1987, and 1986–1973.

## Retrieval and workbook consequence

No figures are added from the index alone. Attempts to open the FY2012 and
FY2011 Companies House PDFs returned a cache miss in the browser environment;
shell DNS/network access is also unavailable. The same limitation applies to
the older filings. The existing workbook remains unchanged by this note.

The next useful sourcing request is therefore not “FY2012 only” but the
following batches, in order:

1. FY2012–FY2008 (the currently assigned range), checking all five filings;
2. FY2007–FY2003, where the name transition to SG Hambros Bank & Trust/SG
   Hambros Bank must be tracked;
3. FY2002–FY1998, checking the FY1998 short-period group filing separately;
4. FY1997–FY1980, with FY1983 treated as a possible transition year;
5. FY1977–FY1973, if the project wants to pursue the older archive.

The account rows are evidence that primary filings exist, not evidence that
their contents are standalone or that a particular metric is disclosed.

### FY1978–FY1979 retrieval finding (2026-09-06)

The later primary-source review found no separate FY1978 or FY1979 annual
accounts entries in the itemised Companies House history. The pre-1995
selection is an appropriate archival route, but its contents are not
enumerated, so it must not be treated as proof that it contains either year.
The two indexed bundle records are:

- https://www.companiesintheuk.co.uk/form/union-bancaire-privee-uk/PRE95/1995-01-01/532917750
- https://www.companiesintheuk.co.uk/form/union-bancaire-privee-uk/PRE95/1995-01-01/532926009

The 1980 accounts supplied locally already provide FY1979 comparative figures
(including total assets £1,380,793k, equity £44,406k and retained earnings
£6,906k), so FY1979 can be sourced defensibly from that primary filing. FY1978
still requires downloading and reading the pre-1995 bundle; it should remain
unverified unless the FY1978 report is actually located.
