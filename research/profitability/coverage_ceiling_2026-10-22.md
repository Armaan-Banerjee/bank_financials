## Reading this

- **The computed year-end rows are the strongest coverage we have** and the
  average-basis rows are not a worse version of them: an average denominator
  needs the prior year, so FY2021 cannot have one. Where both exist they are
  different measures, not different qualities of the same measure.
- **The aggregator rows are the big movable number.** Only FY2024 reaches the
  deliverable today; the collection and the Wayback extraction cover FY2022
  and FY2023 as well, and five banks are lost purely to a name mismatch.
- **The own-published rows will not approach the computed rows** and should
  not be expected to: 66 banks publish no return at all, which is a finding.
  The ceiling there is roughly 72 banks, not 145.
