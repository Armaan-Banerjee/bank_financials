# TheBanks.eu ROA/ROE collection: H–Q

Collected 2026-10-13 and updated 2026-10-14. Scope: all 49 project banks from
**HABIB BANK ZURICH** through **QIB UK**, for TheBanks.eu year labels
2021–2025.

## Result

- Exact-entity coverage: **49/49 banks**.
- Numeric records: **52 bank-years**: FY2021 0, FY2022 1, FY2023 0,
  FY2024 49, FY2025 2.
- Verification: **49** live `thebanks_profile` records and **3** exact-entity
  `search_indexed` historical records.
- ROA is present in all 52 records. ROE is present in 50; TheBanks.eu displays
  no ROE for LHV Bank Limited FY2024 or Perenna Bank PLC FY2024.
- The JSONL contains only explicit numeric figures displayed by TheBanks.eu.
  No ratio was backsolved from the site's balance-sheet or profit data.

The live profile generally exposes only its latest performance year. Historical
year labels were retained only when an exact-entity TheBanks.eu profile result
explicitly displayed the ratio. This produced an indexed FY2022 observation for
Northern Bank and an indexed FY2024 observation for J.P. Morgan Securities
after its live profile advanced to FY2025. A 14 October follow-up also recovered
J.P. Morgan Europe FY2024 ROA 0.12% and ROE 1.43% from explicit retained
profile text after its live page advanced to FY2025. Search results did not
expose further explicit FY2021–FY2023 ratios for this slice; those years are
gaps, not inferred zeros.

## Material cautions

- **HSBC Innovation Bank:** the matched profile identifies company 12546585,
  but displays 2024 assets as GBP9.64 million whereas the audited account trail
  used elsewhere in the project records about GBP9.635 billion. The site's ROA
  and ROE are preserved verbatim and flagged; they were not corrected.
- **Morgan Stanley Bank International:** the project perimeter is company
  03722571 and matches profile `/banks/14577`. A second similarly named profile
  (`/banks/18739`) displays a roughly USD578 billion perimeter and was excluded.
- **Monument Bank:** TheBanks.eu's FY2024 ROE is -23.46%, while the bank's own
  FY2024 report publishes -22%. Both can coexist because source and calculation
  basis differ; neither should silently replace the other.
- **Philippine National Bank (Europe):** TheBanks.eu reports FY2024 ROA 0.07%,
  while the exact bank report publishes 0.1%. The site value is retained as a
  separate calculated-source observation.
- **Kroo:** the -194.60% ROE is extreme but retained exactly. The profile also
  reports a loss and very thin declining equity, so denominator sensitivity is
  material.
- For banks with non-calendar reporting dates (notably Monzo), `FY2024` here
  means TheBanks.eu's displayed `2024` label; it does not independently resolve
  the precise reporting-period endpoint.
