Overview

Business Focus

Crown Agents Bank is a wholesale bank for small and medium-sized countries with the focus on Sub-Saharan Africa, South East Asia and the Caribbean. Crown Agents Bank acts as the prime commercial gateway for central banks, commercial banks, international companies, investors, governments, non-government organisations.

Performance

In 2024, Crown Agents Bank Ltd achieved the position of 101st largest bank in the United Kingdom with a market share of 0.02%; its total assets grew by 4.01%.

Additionally, the bank became the 52nd profitable bank in relation to its total assets showcasing 0.86% return on assets and 10.81% return on equity in 2024.

Also the lender held the 115th position regarging its loans-to-deposits (L/D) registering 13.67% L/D ratio in 2024.

Deposit Protection

Deposits up to 85,000 GBP are covered by the United Kingdom's deposit guarantee scheme in Crown Agents Bank Ltd.
