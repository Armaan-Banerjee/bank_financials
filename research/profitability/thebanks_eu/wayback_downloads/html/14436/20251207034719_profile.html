The Co-operative Bank pioneers banking that makes a positive difference to the lives of our customers and communities. The Co-operative Bank operates as an ethical bank, providing a wide range of retail banking products and services including current accounts, mortgages, savings accounts, credit cards, loans and internet banking to individuals and SMEs.

Performance

In 2024, The Co-operative Bank plc achieved the position of 30th largest bank in the United Kingdom with a market share of 0.35%; its total assets dropped by 2.49%.

Additionally, the bank became the 114th profitable bank in relation to its total assets showcasing 0.18% return on assets and 3.48% return on equity in 2024.

Also the lender held the 36th position regarging its loans-to-deposits (L/D) registering 90.64% L/D ratio in 2024.

Deposit Protection

Deposits up to 85,000 GBP are covered by the United Kingdom's deposit guarantee scheme in The Co-operative Bank plc.

Current Accounts

Current accounts are non-interest bearing deposit accounts that are used to perform day-to-day banking transactions. Current accounts are often offered with a payment card (a debit card and/or a credit card) and may also include overdraft possibility. Annual costs of having a current account (also known as account management fees) can depend on monthly income input to the account or amount of money deposited into the account.
