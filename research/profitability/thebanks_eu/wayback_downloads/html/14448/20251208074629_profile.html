In 2011 Shawbrook’s principal private investor purchased the banking licence of Whiteaway Laidlaw Bank. Shawbrook Bank provides highly personalised, practical lending and savings products to individuals, businesses and real estate investors in the United Kingdom.

Performance

In 2024, Shawbrook Bank Limited achieved the position of 35th largest bank in the United Kingdom with a market share of 0.27%; its total assets grew by 14.78%.

Additionally, the bank became the 33rd profitable bank in relation to its total assets showcasing 1.20% return on assets and 14.65% return on equity in 2024.

Also the lender held the 39th position regarging its loans-to-deposits (L/D) registering 89.53% L/D ratio in 2024.

Deposit Protection

Deposits up to 85,000 GBP are covered by the United Kingdom's deposit guarantee scheme in Shawbrook Bank Limited.
