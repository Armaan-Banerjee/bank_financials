NatWest Markets is a non-ring-fenced investment banking division of NatWest Group. NatWest Markets was created in 2016 from RBS Group's corporate and institutional banking unit. NatWest Markets provides risk management, trading solutions and debt financing to its customers worldwide.

NATWEST MARKETS PLC participates in the deposit guarantee scheme of the United Kingdom. This deposit guarantee scheme applies to current accounts, savings accounts, time deposit accounts made by private individuals, companies and covers up to up to 85,000 GBP per bank per depositor. NATWEST MARKETS PLC is a member of Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) and makes its contributions into it.
