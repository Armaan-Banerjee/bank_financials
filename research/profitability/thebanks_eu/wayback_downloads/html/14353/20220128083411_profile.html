Hampshire Trust Bank is a UK bank formed in 1977. Hampshire Trust Bank is focused on providing asset finance, commercial finance and property finance to the UK customers. Hampshire Trust Bank also provides savings accounts to individuals and businesses.

Hampshire Trust Bank plc is the 96th largest bank in the United Kingdom in terms of total assets. In 2020 its total assets were 1 522,36 mln GBP, providing the bank with the market share of 0.02%. In 2020 the bank's annual profit was 2,55 mln GBP. Hampshire Trust Bank plc had 216 employees in 2020.

Hampshire Trust Bank plc participates in the deposit guarantee scheme of the United Kingdom. This deposit guarantee scheme applies to current accounts, savings accounts, time deposit accounts made by private individuals, companies and covers up to up to 85,000 GBP per bank per depositor. Hampshire Trust Bank plc is a member of Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) and makes its contributions into it.
