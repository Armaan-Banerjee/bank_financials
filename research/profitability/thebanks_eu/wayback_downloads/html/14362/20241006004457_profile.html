Business Focus

Established in 1950, Investec Bank is a British financial institution focused on providing specialist banking, asset and wealth management in three principal markets, the United Kingdom, South Africa and Australia, as well as certain other geographies.

Performance

In 2022, Investec Bank Plc achieved the position of 28th largest bank in the United Kingdom with a market share of 0.27%; its total assets grew by 3.78%.

Additionally, the bank became the 41st profitable bank in relation to its total assets showcasing 0.94% return on assets and 10.23% return on equity in 2022.

Also the lender held the 87th position regarging its loans-to-deposits (L/D) registering 58.79% L/D ratio in 2022.

Deposit Protection

Deposits up to 85,000 GBP are covered by the United Kingdom's deposit guarantee scheme in Investec Bank Plc.
