# Profitability coverage gaps — 2026-10-15

Source: `UK_banks_profitability_2026-10-15.csv`, 145 banks x 5 years = 725 bank-years.

Generated by `gap_report.py`. **Four sources, four answers — never merged.**
A bank-year with no computed ratio is a different claim from one where the
bank publishes no return, which is different again from one an aggregator
does not carry.

Reading it:

- **The two average-basis rows are not measuring absent data.** An average
  denominator needs the prior year, so FY2021 needs FY2020, which is outside
  the window. Compare each against the year-end row immediately below it:
  FY2021 ROE is 55 missing on the average basis and 9 on year-end, and the
  year-end figure IS published for those banks.
- **The statement-figures row is the real measure of our reach**, since every
  computed ratio derives from it: 17 absences in 725 bank-years, none of them
  mid-series.
- **The last three rows are other people's publishing decisions, not our
  coverage.** A bank that publishes no return cannot be missing one, and 66
  banks publish none at all — so most of the own-published row is a finding.
  The aggregator row is near-total by nature: the site exposes only the
  current performance year.

### Publishes a return, but NOT for any year in the window

A distinct kind of gap: the measure exists for this bank, just not for
FY2021-FY2025. Two of these stopped publishing before the window opened,
which is a finding about the bank, not a hole in our reach.

- **ATOM BANK** — publishes only FY2026
- **BANK OF BEIRUT UK** — publishes only FY2016, FY2017
- **ZENITH** — publishes only FY2019, FY2020

This is why two counts of "banks that publish" are both correct and must
be labelled: **72** publish a return somewhere, **69** publish one inside
the FY2021-FY2025 window.

## 4. Aggregator (thebanks.eu)

Reaching the deliverable: **20** bank-years, all FY2024.

**5 aggregator banks never reach it because the name does not match**, so
their values are silently dropped. The aggregator file states the entity for
each, and every one resolves unambiguously:

| aggregator name | entity it states | deliverable name |
|---|---|---|
| HAMPSHIRE TRUST | Hampshire Trust Bank plc (United Kingdom) | HAMPSHIRE TRUST BANK |
| HSBC UK BANK | HSBC UK Bank Plc (United Kingdom) | HSBC UK BANK PLC |
| NATWEST | National Westminster Bank (United Kingdom) | NATIONAL WESTMINSTER BANK PLC |
| OAKNORTH | Oaknorth Bank PLC (United Kingdom) | OAKNORTH BANK |
| SANTANDER UK | Santander UK (United Kingdom) | SANTANDER |

Note the two that look ambiguous and are not: the collector recorded `NOT NatWest Group plc` and
`NOT Santander UK Group Holdings`, which is what settles them.

Separately, `thebanks_eu/thebanks_eu_returns_with_archives.jsonl` holds **149
observations across 141 entities** and is NOT wired into the deliverable. Adopting it
is an open decision, not an oversight.
