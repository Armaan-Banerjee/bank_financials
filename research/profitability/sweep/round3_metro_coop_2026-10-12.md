## Metro Bank PLC — resolved

### Decisive evidence

The exact Companies House filing is [Metro Bank PLC Annual Report and Accounts 2025, document `MzUzODAzNTg0NWFkaXF6a2N4`](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/06419578/filing-history/MzUzODAzNTg0NWFkaXF6a2N4/document?format=pdf&download=0), filed 13 August 2026 (163 pages). A [faithful preserved copy of that original PDF](https://cdn.financialreports.eu/financialreports/media/filings/4947/2026/RNS/4947_rns_2026-08-13_fc2af718-accf-4f50-a989-0ac163d4dea1.pdf#page=12) was located through the preserved filing index, whose cover identifies **“METRO BANK PLC 06419578”** and whose contents place “Key performance indicators” on printed p.11 ([preserved filing index](https://financialfilings.com/filings/metro-bank-plc/annual-report/2026/57189458/)).

Printed p.11 (PDF page 12; rendered and visually read at source resolution) explicitly reports **Return on tangible equity (%)** as:

- FY2025: **5**
- FY2024: **(22)**
- FY2023: **7**

The same page defines the metric as statutory profit after tax attributable to shareholders divided by average tangible equity, where tangible equity excludes other equity instruments, intangible assets and deferred tax assets. The FY2025 value to record is therefore **RoTE = 5%**. This is a direct transcription of the published KPI, not a reconstruction.

### Exact perimeter

This is the **Metro Bank PLC consolidated group**, not the Company-only/standalone column and not Metro Bank Holdings PLC. The filing says the report is for the “consolidated Group”; its auditor separately distinguishes the Group and Company financial statements; and note 7 identifies the Company's two wholly owned subsidiaries as SME Invoice Finance Limited and SME Asset Finance Limited ([same preserved filing](https://financialfilings.com/filings/metro-bank-plc/annual-report/2026/57189458/)). Accordingly, the 5% KPI is compatible only with a bank-consolidated Metro Bank PLC perimeter.

### Explicit exclusion

The official [Metro Bank Holdings PLC Annual Report and Accounts 2025](https://www.metrobankonline.co.uk/globalassets/documents/investor_documents/metro-bank---annual-report-2025.pdf#page=15) reports parent-group RoTE of 6%, (23)% and 4% for FY2025–FY2023. Those values were **not** used: that report is headed Metro Bank Holdings PLC, whereas the requested entity is company 06419578, and the exact bank filing reports a different statutory PBT (£90.9m versus £87.2m at Holdings), confirming a real perimeter difference.

## The Co-operative Bank p.l.c. — remains unresolved

### Exact filing and sources checked

Companies House records an 81-page group-accounts filing for the year ended 31 December 2025: [The Co-operative Bank p.l.c., company 00990937, document `MzUxOTQwMjIzNGFkaXF6a2N4`](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/00990937/filing-history/MzUxOTQwMjIzNGFkaXF6a2N4/document?format=pdf&download=0), filed 4 May 2026 ([filing history](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/00990937/filing-history)). The document endpoint continued to redirect to an expired/inaccessible signed Companies House object during this sweep, and no faithful preserved original of the 81-page PDF was located on the Bank site, FCA/NSM, issuer mirrors or indexed archives.

The Bank's [official financial-results archive](https://www.co-operativebank.co.uk/about-us/investor-relations/financial-results/) publishes the FY2025 year-end [Pillar 3 disclosure](https://www.co-operativebank.co.uk/pdfs/bank/investorrelations/2025-h2-pillar-3-disclosures.pdf), but does not publish a FY2025 Bank annual report; full-document searches of that Pillar 3 disclosure found no “return on assets”, “return on equity” or profitability KPI. The archive transitions directly from 2025 Pillar 3 / half-year materials to the 2024 ARA.

The Bank's current [CRD V compliance page](https://www.co-operativebank.co.uk/about-us/our-business/capital-requirements-directive-compliance/) states that the Bank publishes return on assets among annual-report key indicators and defines it as net profit divided by total balance sheet. It supplies **no numeric FY2025 value**, so it establishes only the metric and formula and cannot support a record.

### Values deliberately excluded

Coventry Building Society's [2025 Annual Report and Accounts](https://www.coventrybuildingsociety.co.uk/content/dam/cbs/member/pdfs/financial-results/2025/Annual-Report-and-Accounts-2025.pdf) covers the enlarged group after its 1 January 2025 acquisition of The Co-operative Bank Holdings p.l.c.; it says FY2025 includes the acquisition's financial impacts and that comparative periods exclude the Bank (printed p.2). Its annual business statement reports “Net profit as a percentage of total Balance Sheet” of 0.92% for 2025 (printed p.198), but that is a **Coventry enlarged-group** statutory percentage, not The Co-operative Bank p.l.c. or the Bank Group. It is therefore excluded.

Likewise, the exact Bank filing's published statement values (including net profit and total assets reproduced by company-data mirrors) were not divided to create an ROA. The requested rule requires an explicitly published ratio.

### Conclusion

No admissible FY2025 ROA, ROE or RoTE can currently be recorded for The Co-operative Bank p.l.c. The gap can be closed only by obtaining the exact 81-page Companies House PDF (or a faithful preserved original) and finding an explicit numeric ratio within it; absent that, the result must remain null rather than be backsolved or borrowed from Coventry Building Society.
