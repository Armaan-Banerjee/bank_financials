# Profitability unresolved retry, round 3: GSIB and PNBE (2026-10-12)

## Scope and outcome

This is a collection-only retry for **Goldman Sachs International Bank**
(GSIB) and **Philippine National Bank (Europe) Plc** (PNBE). It applies the
published-returns convention: exact legal entity, an explicit published
ROE/ROA/RoTE value, and no backsolving or parent substitution.

**Neither bank can yet be promoted from `unresolved`.** The exact FY2025
accounts exist, but their Companies House page images still cannot be read by
the available route. The extra primary-source checks below narrow the gap; they
do not justify a negative publication finding.

No worksheet, curated store, extract, CSV/XLSX or deliverable was changed.

## Goldman Sachs International Bank

- Companies House identifies company **01122503** as Goldman Sachs
  International Bank and lists its 64-page full accounts made up to
  31 December 2025, filed 26 April 2026: [official filing
  history](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/01122503/filing-history),
  [exact document](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/01122503/filing-history/MzUxNzc2NzE5OGFkaXF6a2N4/document?format=pdf&download=0).
- The exact document redirects to a short-lived Companies House signed object
  which the available browser and shell routes cannot open. The local project
  cache also contains no copy of this 64-page filing. Consequently the annual
  report still cannot be searched in full or inspected page-by-page.
- The official Goldman Sachs Group UK Pillar 3 publication is the right
  prudential shelf and includes GSIB entity columns for regulatory metrics:
  [Q4 2025 Pillar 3](https://www.goldmansachs.com/disclosures/gsguk-q4-2025-pillar-3.pdf/).
  Its return terminology occurs in the remuneration section as a list of
  firmwide performance measures considered in compensation decisions; it does
  not expose a numeric GSIB ROE, ROA or RoTE. The directly searchable prior
  edition shows the same context: [Q4 2024 Pillar
  3](https://www.goldmansachs.com/disclosures/gsguk-q4-2024-pillar-3.pdf).
- Searches also locate numeric return measures for **The Goldman Sachs Group,
  Inc.** and the annual report of **Goldman Sachs International**. Both are
  different reporting perimeters and are excluded.

**Remaining evidence needed:** the 64-page FY2025 GSIB accounts themselves,
preferably the original PDF or page images, for whole-report return-term search
and visual confirmation.

**Remaining evidence needed:** the 45-page FY2025 PNBE accounts themselves,

## Collection decision

Keep the existing single unresolved FY2025 record for each bank. Do not convert
either record to `publishes_none`: a successful search of a complementary
Pillar 3 document is not a substitute for reading the annual accounts to which
that document points.
