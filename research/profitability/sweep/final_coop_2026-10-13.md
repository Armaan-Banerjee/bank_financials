# The Co-operative Bank p.l.c. FY2025 profitability review

Date checked: 13 October 2026

## Outcome

**Close as `publishes_none`.** The exact FY2025 Annual Report and Accounts for The Co-operative Bank p.l.c. publishes no numeric return on equity, return on assets, return on tangible equity, or synonymous bank-profitability return measure. No value should be calculated from the reported profit and balance-sheet figures, and no Coventry Building Society group measure should be substituted.

## Exact source and perimeter

The reviewed primary source is the uploaded copy of the official [Companies House filing for The Co-operative Bank p.l.c.](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/00990937/filing-history/MzUxOTQwMjIzNGFkaXF6a2N4/document?format=pdf&download=0), Annual Report and Accounts for the year ended 31 December 2025. Its cover identifies **The Co-operative Bank p.l.c.**, registered company number **00990937**, and the 31 December 2025 year end (PDF p.1). The file is an 81-page image-only Companies House scan; SHA-256: `0744a776296bc7565ef3cfafa1fcbd66f9880bcbe1f740e49e994ee1773a274d`.

The report distinguishes:

- **the Company / Bank**: The Co-operative Bank p.l.c.; and
- **the Bank Group**: the group headed by The Co-operative Bank p.l.c., including all its subsidiaries (PDF p.2).

The strategic report presents the consolidated results of that Bank Group (printed p.9; PDF p.10). This is the exact bank legal entity and its own statutory consolidation, not Coventry Building Society's enlarged Group.

## Decisive evidence

The report's financial-review and KPI sequence was checked visually from 300 dpi renders:

- Printed p.9 (PDF p.10) reports the 2025/2024 summary income statement and statutory/underlying profit before tax, but no ROE, ROA or RoTE.
- Printed p.10 (PDF p.11) reports the summary balance sheet and regulatory capital ratios, but no profitability return ratio.
- Printed p.11 (PDF p.12), headed **Key performance indicators**, expressly lists the Bank Group's financial KPIs as statutory and underlying profit before tax, CET1 ratio, LCR and customer balance growth. It does not list or publish ROE, ROA, RoTE or another return-to-equity/assets measure.

That KPI page also directs readers to Coventry Building Society's Group ARA for **combined** Group performance. Any return measure there would have the wrong perimeter and is therefore excluded.

## Whole-report search and false positive

Because the source has no embedded text, all 81 pages were rendered and OCR'd twice using different page-segmentation modes. The resulting full-report text was searched for `return on equity`, `return on assets`, `return on tangible equity`, `ROE`, `ROA`, `RoTE`, `tangible equity`, `average equity`, `average assets`, `shareholder return`, `return on capital`, and risk-adjusted-return variants.

The only apparent `return on assets` hit is in note 23, the pension-scheme reconciliation (printed p.57; PDF p.58). The table records **Return on assets** of £(32.0)m for 2025 and £(252.6)m for 2024 as movements in the fair value of defined-benefit **plan assets**. A second table calls this **Return on plan assets**. These are monetary pension remeasurement amounts, not the Bank's ROA percentage, and were visually confirmed at 300 dpi before exclusion.
