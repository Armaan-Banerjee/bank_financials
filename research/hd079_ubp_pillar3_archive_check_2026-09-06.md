# HD-079 UBP UK Pillar 3 archive check (2026-09-06)

## Conclusion

No additional UK-entity Pillar 3 capital/RWA series was found for FY2014–FY2021.
The official UBP UK legal-aspects page currently publishes only the SG Kleinwort
Hambros disclosures for 31 December 2022, 2023 and 2024. The 2022 report is a
genuine prudential disclosure, but it is prepared at **UK Consolidation Group**
level, incorporating SGKH Bank Ltd, its branches and the trust entity—not a
bank-solo basis. It is therefore unsuitable as a historical substitute for a
missing standalone series without an explicit basis label.

Sources:

- [UBP UK Pillar 3 page](https://www.ubp.com/en/legal-aspects/union-bancaire-privee-uk-limited/pillar-3-disclosure) — lists only 2022–2024 UK reports.
- [SGKH 2022 Pillar 3 report](https://www.ubp.com/files/live/sites/ubp/files/documents/legal/ubp-uk/UBP_2022_P3_disclosures.pdf) — identifies the UK Consolidation Group scope and includes KM1, OV1, own funds and liquidity disclosures.
- [SGKH 2023 report](https://www.ubp.com/files/live/sites/ubp/files/documents/legal/ubp-uk/UBP_2023_P3_disclosures.pdf) — repeats the UK-consolidation scope and the prudential disclosure framework.

## Remuneration-only distinction

Article 450 remuneration disclosures are not a substitute for capital, RWA,
leverage, LCR or NSFR Pillar 3 data. The current SGKH report places remuneration
in a separate annex and identifies it as Article 450 information; its core
prudential sections are the KM1/OV1/own-funds/risk sections. Any older search
hit consisting only of remuneration information should therefore not populate
the workbook’s Pillar 3 metrics.
