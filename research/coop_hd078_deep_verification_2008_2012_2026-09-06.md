# Co-operative Bank HD-078 deep verification: FY2008–FY2012

Scope: independent review of the pre-2014 statutory statement coverage, with particular attention to blanks in the Profit & Loss sheet and whether the cited reports actually support the stated basis. Sources reviewed were the Bank's official 2012 Financial Statements and official 2013 Annual Report (both searchable full PDFs), plus the official 2010 Financial Statements.

## Material finding: FY2012/FY2013 P&L blanks are recoverable

The current builder leaves the following rows blank for FY2012 and FY2013 while saying that the source does not itemise them consistently. The Bank's 2013 Annual Report does in fact disclose them directly in **The Bank income statement**, p.128:

| Workbook row | FY2013 | FY2012 | Source |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Net fee and commission income | -37.3 | 27.5 | 2013 AR p.128 |
| Other operating income/(expense) | 40.4 | 33.9 | 2013 AR p.128 |
| Total operating expenses | -1,029.1 | -847.5 | 2013 AR p.128 |
| Operating (loss) before impairment | -759.0 | -206.2 | 2013 AR p.128 |
| Net impairment gains/(losses) on loans and advances | -516.2 | -474.1 | 2013 AR p.128 |
| Operating (loss) (post-LME for FY2013) | -586.9 | -674.9 | 2013 AR p.128 |
| Share of post-tax profits from joint ventures | 0.7 | 1.2 | 2013 AR p.128 |
| Profit/(loss) attributable to equity shareholders | -748.9 | -509.1 | 2013 AR p.128 |

The same page also gives fee/commission income before provisions (219.9/241.4), PPI/redress provisions (-185.1/-149.7), fee expense (-72.1/-64.2), trading income (3.8/14.1), and the full operating-loss bridge. These are direct Bank figures, not Group figures. The official PDF's searchable text confirms the table and values: https://www.co-operativebank.co.uk/pdfs/bank/investorrelations/financialresults/bank-r-and-a.pdf (p.128, PDF page 128; searchable lines 7653–7692 in the web rendering).

The current workbook already has FY2013/FY2012 net interest, operating income, pre-tax loss, tax, and loss-for-year values, but the above direct lines should be added. This is a genuine omission, not a historical extension.

## OCI lines also recoverable for FY2012/FY2013

The 2013 Annual Report p.129 gives the Bank's statement of comprehensive income for both years. Equity-shareholder attributable OCI is **FY2013 £6.5m** and **FY2012 £6.5m**; total comprehensive income attributable to equity shareholders is **FY2013 -£841.8m** and **FY2012 -£502.6m**. The source gives components, including cash-flow hedges, available-for-sale assets, equity-share revaluation, and tax. The current P&L rows 20–24 are blank for these years and should be populated on the workbook's established equity-shareholder basis, with the minority-interest distinction documented. Source: 2013 AR p.129, lines 7698–7742 in the web rendering.

## FY2011 bottom-line and OCI are recoverable

The official 2012 Financial Statements p.40 include the Bank column for FY2011. It discloses:

- Bank total comprehensive income for FY2011: **-£30.1m**;
- Bank retained-earnings movement: **-£93.4m**;
- Bank available-for-sale reserve movement: **+£32.6m**;
- Bank cash-flow-hedging reserve movement: **+£30.7m**;
- Bank closing equity: **£2,160.4m**.

The same report's note 11 p.91 says the Bank's profit attributable to equity shareholders for FY2011 was **-£93.3m** after significant items (the six-column Group/Bank reconciliation gives Bank figures; the source explicitly says the individual Bank income statement was not presented under s.408). Therefore FY2011 can safely populate the existing bottom-line/OCI rows, but not the full P&L detail. Source: official 2012 PDF p.40 and p.91, https://www.co-operativebank.co.uk/pdfs/bank/investorrelations/financialresults/bank-financial-statement-2012.pdf (web rendering lines 3313–3379 and 6013–6037).

## FY2010/FY2009 basis confirmed

The official 2010 Financial Statements p.37 show Bank total comprehensive income FY2010 **£56.0m** and FY2009 **£165.4m**, with the Bank-specific reserve movements. Note 11 p.90 gives Bank profit attributable to equity shareholders FY2010 **£73.1m** and FY2009 **£166.6m**, and explicitly states that the separate Bank income statement was not presented under s.408. Thus full P&L detail for FY2009–FY2010 should remain blank, but bottom-line/OCI rows can be populated if the workbook has not already represented them through the equity roll-forward. Source: https://www.co-operativebank.co.uk/assets/pdf/bank/investorrelations/financialresults/bank-financial-statement-2010.pdf (p.37 and p.90; web rendering lines 3539–3557 and 6347–6371).

## FY2012 restatement warning

The Bank's own 2012 report gives Bank profit attributable to equity shareholders of **-£588.2m** (note 11 p.91) and Bank total comprehensive income **-£581.5m** (p.40). The 2013 Annual Report's Bank income statement gives FY2012 comparative profit attributable to equity shareholders **-£509.1m** and total comprehensive income **-£502.6m** (p.128–129). The workbook currently uses the 2013 comparative values and separately flags the £221.1m equity restatement, but does not explicitly flag this P&L comparative change. Both are source-disclosed figures on the same Bank basis; neither should be silently overwritten. Add a dedicated source/restatement note or preserve both via an explicit reconciliation.

## FY2012 balance sheet source check

The official 2012 Bank balance sheet p.37 agrees with the workbook's own-year values (total assets **£51,818.5m**, total liabilities **£50,189.4m**, total equity **£1,629.1m**). The 2013 report's restated FY2012 comparative p.130 instead gives total assets **£49,772.8m** and total equity **£1,850.2m**, with notes explaining restatements for intangible assets, PPE, intercompany amounts and deferred tax presentation. Existing workbook treatment of these as separate source/restatement positions is appropriate.

## Conclusion

The main actionable recovery is the FY2012/FY2013 full Bank P&L and OCI detail, plus FY2011–FY2009 bottom-line/OCI rows. FY2008 should remain constrained to the existing balance sheet, SORIE/equity and cash-flow coverage unless the underlying scanned 2008 filing is separately OCR-reviewed; no web-accessible primary text was found during this pass.
