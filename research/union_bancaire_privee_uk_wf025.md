# WF-025 research — Union Bancaire Privée (UK) Limited

Research date: 2026-08-29. Primary-source research for the assigned bank.

## Entity and reporting basis

The assigned entity is Union Bancaire Privée (UK) Limited, Companies House
number 00964058 and FRN 119250. It was formerly SG Kleinwort Hambros Bank
Limited. Companies House records the name change in April 2025 following the
acquisition by Union Bancaire Privée, UBP SA on 1 April 2025.

The official Pillar 3 disclosures for 2022–2024 are still titled SG Kleinwort
Hambros Bank Limited because they describe the pre-acquisition reporting
periods. Each states that the prudential disclosures are prepared at the **UK
Consolidation Group** level, incorporating SG Kleinwort Hambros Bank Limited,
its branches, and the trust entity. This is not a bank-solo dataset and is
labelled accordingly throughout the workbook.

The disclosures state that the published financial statements use FRS 101
and do not consolidate the relevant subsidiaries, while the prudential scope
consolidates the UK group. The workbook is therefore Pillar-3-only and does
not import cash flows from the accounting statements or another entity.

## Coverage and figures

The 2022 disclosure supplies FY2022 and FY2021 comparative columns; the 2023
disclosure supplies FY2023 and FY2022 comparatives; and the 2024 disclosure
supplies FY2024 and FY2023 comparatives. To preserve each year's own annual
disclosure basis, the workbook uses the current-year column from the relevant
edition and the FY2022 disclosure's FY2021 comparative.
