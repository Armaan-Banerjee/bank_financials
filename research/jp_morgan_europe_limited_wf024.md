# WF-024 research — J.P. Morgan Europe Limited

Research date: 2026-08-29. Primary-source research for the JPMEL workbook.

## Entity and basis

The assigned entity is **J.P. MORGAN EUROPE LIMITED**, Companies House number
00938937. The Companies House filing history identifies the entity and shows
full accounts through 31 December 2025. JPMorgan's legal-entity information
also identifies J.P. Morgan Europe Limited as a UK-regulated legal entity.

The workbook uses JPMEL's own UK Pillar 3 tables, in GBP millions, not
JPMorgan Chase group, JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A., JPMorgan Securities plc, or
JPMorgan SE figures. The 2024 JPMEL annual accounts state that the financial
statements use FRS 101 Reduced Disclosure Framework. The absence of a
cash-flow statement is therefore recorded as an exemption rather than filled
from group accounts.

## Pillar 3 coverage

- FY2025: official JPMorgan UK **P3 Annual Solo 2025**, Table 2 UK KM1 for
  JPMEL and related standalone tables.
- FY2024: official **P3 Annual Solo 2024**, Table 2 UK KM1 for JPMEL.
- FY2023: the FY2024 JPMEL KM1 comparative column.
- FY2021–FY2022: no JPMEL standalone KM1/annual solo figures were found in
  the official archive. The available 2022/2021 documents are consolidated
  or identify other JPMorgan entities, so they are not substituted.

Build a 13-sheet Pillar-3-only workbook. The cash-flow sheet documents the
FRS 101 exemption and has no fabricated values. Annual standalone Pillar 3
metrics are populated for FY2023–FY2025; FY2021–FY2022 and MREL are marked
“Not publicly disclosed”.
