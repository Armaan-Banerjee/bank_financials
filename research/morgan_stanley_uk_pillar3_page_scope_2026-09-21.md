# Morgan Stanley UK Pillar 3 page: entity scope and coverage

Checked 2026-09-21 against Morgan Stanley's official UK Pillar 3 index and its FY2025 documents, prompted by the possibility that the page supplies disclosures for Morgan Stanley Bank International Limited (MSBIL).

## Finding

The page is a valuable official source index and does link to comprehensive current UK Pillar 3 reporting. However, the annual Pillar 3 report is prepared for the **Morgan Stanley International Limited Group (MSI Group)**, not MSBIL as a standalone entity. The report separately identifies only MSIP (Morgan Stanley & Co. International plc) as a large subsidiary with its own subsidiary-level KM1 disclosure. It does not provide an MSBIL-specific KM1 or a standalone MSBIL metric set. The page does not, therefore, remove MSBIL-specific gaps by itself, and group/MSIP figures should not be copied into the MSBIL workbook.

The FY2025 Pillar 3 report's own scope statement says the report is based on the consolidated situation of MSI Group and that separate subsidiary disclosures are required for MSIP and MSESE; it identifies MSIP as a large subsidiary (printed p.5). Its UK KM1 table is for MSI Group (printed p.6), followed by a separate KM1 for MSIP (printed p.7). The report's contents identify UK OV1 as “MSI Group and MSIP” (printed p.3). MSBIL is not identified as a separate Pillar 3 reporting perimeter in these tables.

Morgan Stanley's page states that the UK disclosures are for MSI and its subsidiaries (“MSI Group”) and calls MSI Group the main regulated Morgan Stanley group in the UK. The page indexes annual reports from 2017 onward, quarterly reports in recent years, a separate CRD IV country-by-country report, and other entity-specific disclosures. The linked FY2025 CRD IV country-by-country document is not a substitute for bank-level prudential metrics: its tables report turnover, profit before tax, tax and employee totals by geography, plus a list of legal entities, not own-funds/RWA/KM1 figures.

## Workbook implication

The relevant workbook is Morgan Stanley Bank International Limited, not Morgan Stanley International Limited Group or Morgan Stanley & Co. International plc. The current builder already takes MSBIL's own-entity capital, ratio, RWA, leverage and liquidity values from its Annual Reports; its KM1 sheet correctly notes that the UK MSI Pillar 3 disclosures are group-level. This page is now recorded as a checked source lead, but no MSBIL figures were found here to ingest and no workbook cells were changed.
