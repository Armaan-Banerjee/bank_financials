# J.P. Morgan Securities plc — revisit research

## Conclusion

The existing workbook could be defensibly expanded from FY2024–FY2025 capital/leverage coverage to a full FY2021–FY2025 capital, RWA, leverage and LCR series. The official JPMorgan archive exposes standalone annual Pillar 3 reports for each of those years. NSFR is directly supported from FY2022 onward. No standalone numeric MREL ratio or requirement is reported, so the MREL sheet remains unavailable rather than being inferred from subordinated capital instruments.

## Entity and basis

The reports identify J.P. Morgan Securities plc (JPMS plc) as a primary subsidiary of J.P. Morgan Capital Holdings Limited and a PRA/FCA-regulated UK entity. The quantitative tables are expressly labelled for JPMS plc and are presented in USD because USD is its functional currency. The workbook preserves that entity-level basis and converts monetary values to GBP at the established Bank of England GBP/USD year-end spot rates; percentage ratios remain as reported.

Sources: [2021 report](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/static-files/41a50bf4-81db-4063-8e55-8703a0fc500f), [2022 report](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/static-files/d761ba10-ab1b-4309-8a4d-24cb70fd6cc1), [2023 report](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/static-files/3645e9a4-c721-404e-a901-70fa73fac642), [2024 report](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/static-files/a170e6df-d951-42fc-9897-2aa04d681885), and [2025 report](https://jpmorganchaseco.gcs-web.com/static-files/a51475c0-d908-423e-b8de-6410d277a867).

## Liquidity

LCR is directly supported for all five years. FY2021's older disclosure reports liquidity buffer, total net cash outflows and LCR but does not separately report cash-outflow and cash-inflow components; those two FY2021 cells are intentionally blank. FY2022–FY2025 provide the full component rows.

NSFR is directly supported for FY2022–FY2025. The 2021 report contains no NSFR series and the 2022 report provides no FY2021 comparative, so FY2021 NSFR cells are explicitly marked not publicly disclosed.

## MREL and cash flow

The reports describe the MREL framework and identify subordinated instruments issued in support of the Firm's MREL strategy, but do not state a JPMS plc numeric MREL ratio or requirement. The workbook therefore does not derive one from Tier 2 capital.

JPMS plc's annual accounts use the FRS 101 reduced-disclosure framework and take the IAS 7 cash-flow-statement exemption. The workbook remains Pillar-3-only with the exemption documented on the Cash Flow Statement sheet.

## Before/after

- Before: FY2024–FY2025 capital, RWA and leverage populated; FY2021–FY2023 and LCR/NSFR/MREL unavailable.
- After: FY2021–FY2025 capital, RWA, leverage and LCR populated; FY2022–FY2025 NSFR populated; MREL remains explicitly unavailable.
