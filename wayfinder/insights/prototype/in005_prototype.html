Key takeaways

Distinct signals from the available year-on-year comparisons.

Risk-weighted assets generally expanded: RWA increased for 73 of 99 comparable entities from FY2022 to FY2023 and 75 of 100 from FY2023 to FY2024. This is an amount, not a capital-ratio percentage.

Capital ratios turned more defensive after FY2022→FY2023: CET1 increased for 58 of 109 entities in FY2022→FY2023, but declined for 61 of 108 in FY2023→FY2024 and 61 of 92 in FY2024→FY2025.

Operating cash flow was cyclical: 70 of 118 comparable entities improved in FY2023→FY2024, followed by declines for 62 of 104 in FY2024→FY2025.

Metric guide
  • CET1 ratio: highest-quality capital ÷ risk-weighted assets.
  • Tier 1 ratio: core and additional Tier 1 capital ÷ risk-weighted assets.
  • Total capital ratio: total regulatory capital, including Tier 2 where applicable, ÷ risk-weighted assets.
  • Leverage ratio: Tier 1 capital ÷ total exposure, without risk weighting.
  • LCR: high-quality liquid assets ÷ stressed 30-day net cash outflows; it is not directly comparable in scale with capital ratios.
  • NSFR: available stable funding ÷ required stable funding over a longer horizon.
  • MREL ratio: loss-absorbing and recapitalisation resources relative to the applicable exposure measure; coverage is sparse.
  • RWA: risk-weighted assets, an amount rather than a percentage; source currencies and bases vary.
  • Operating cash flow: net cash generated by or used in operating activities; reporting bases vary.
