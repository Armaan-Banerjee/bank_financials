## Question

Can the source-of-truth database feed a maintainable Power BI model without
duplicating business rules in report visuals?

## Scope

- Define/export fact observations plus entity, metric, period, parent-group,
  source, unit, comparability, and quality dimensions.
- Publish explicit measures and field definitions for level, movement, rank,
  coverage, and sample size.
- Document refresh, schema-change, lineage, and validation contracts.
- Provide a small importable hand-off (SQLite/CSV views or equivalent); a
  `.pbix` is optional and must not become the only source of truth.

## Completion criteria

- A Power BI author can build visuals without copying SQL or label logic.
- Measures reconcile with the HTML/PDF outputs on a fixture and real refresh.
- Period, unit, reporting basis, and missingness remain filterable dimensions.

## Progress

Completed on 2026-08-30. Added `in018_powerbi.py`, which exports a reproducible
CSV star schema from `research/insights.db`: an annual observation fact at
`FRN × metric sheet × source row label × reported period`, plus bank, metric,
period, source, unit, reporting-basis, and quality dimensions. Parent-group
attributes remain on the bank dimension so they can be filtered without
duplicating entity rows.

Added `semantic_model.json` with relationship cardinalities, explicit DAX
measures for numeric observations, reported values, coverage, missingness,
comparable N, and latest year, plus refresh, schema-change, lineage, and
authoring rules. The contract instructs report authors to use IN-009/IN-016
findings rather than recreating comparability or outlier logic in visuals.

The export is now a standard refresh step before deliverable generation and is
covered by fixture tests for fact grain, stable dimension keys, relationships,
and measure definitions. The live refresh produces 12,846 fact observations,
145 bank entities, 12 metric entities, 7 periods, 145 source entities, and
filterable unit/comparability/quality dimensions. A `.pbix` was intentionally
not created; SQLite and the CSV/JSON hand-off remain authoritative.

Resolution: IN-018 completion criteria met. Future report interaction and
regulatory context belong in IN-019 and IN-020.
