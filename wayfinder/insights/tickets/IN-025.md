## Question

Where is the evidence strong enough for a cross-bank conclusion, and where
does disclosure quality limit interpretation?

## Scope

- Build bank × metric × year coverage and quality heatmaps for annual data.
- Add cadence summaries for annual, semi-annual, quarterly, point-in-time,
  missing, and non-numeric states.
- Add a source-trace table linking each observation to FRN, workbook, row
  label, raw value, source note, unit, basis, and quality status.
- Keep interim data separate from annual cohorts and show its limited coverage
  as context, not as a sector-wide trend.
- Add visible uncertainty badges to the other new visuals: N, basis/unit
  coverage, and unresolved quality flags.

## Completion criteria

- Views are generated from SQLite and refresh deterministically.
- HTML provides searchable source trace and accessible tables; PDF provides a
  compact evidence-quality summary.
- Missing data is never rendered as zero, and non-numeric disclosure is not
  silently treated as missing numeric data.
- Tests cover quality counts, annual/interim separation, FRN joins, and trace
  completeness; full tests and refresh remain green.

## Resolution

Implemented 2026-08-30. Added `in025_quality_views.py` with deterministic
annual/interim cadence summaries, explicit quality states, bank × metric ×
