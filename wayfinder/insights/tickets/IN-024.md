## Question

Which banks show persistent movement rather than a single-year change or
sample-coverage churn?

## Scope

- Build bank-level adjacent-year change fingerprints and persistence scores
  for the core ratios.
- Add HTML trajectory small multiples, a direction/magnitude heatmap, and a
  fixed-panel percentile-rank slopegraph.
- Use fixed eligible panels for rank comparisons and a separate coverage view
  for banks entering or leaving the sample.
- Rank review candidates by persistence and robust total change, with source
  and comparability drillthrough.

## Completion criteria

- Persistence, rank, and eligibility rules are deterministic and tested.
- Missing years are explicit and every view states its panel definition and N.
- HTML supports filtering; PDF includes a concise candidate summary and table.
- No bank is labelled weak, improving, or deteriorating from an insufficient
  series; full tests and refresh remain green.
