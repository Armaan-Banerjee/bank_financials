## Question

What does a bank's level or movement look like relative to a comparable
population, beyond the current two-cluster and up/down summaries?

## Scope

- Add robust median, quartiles/IQR, percentile rank, MAD/robust z-score, and
  cohort-size statistics for explicitly comparable populations.
- Add rank movement and distribution views for ratios, absolute values, and
  trends, with parent-group and peer-group filters.
- Attach denominator, coverage, and minimum-sample warnings to every result.
- Keep statistical association separate from causal language; document any
  confidence interval or bootstrap policy selected.

## Completion criteria

- Results are reproducible from SQLite and tested against hand-checkable
  fixtures.
- Unknown units, mixed bases, and insufficient cohorts cannot enter a shared
  distribution silently.
- HTML/PDF receive concise summaries and accessible table fallbacks.

## Progress

Completed on 2026-08-30. Added `in016_distribution.py` with reproducible,
SQLite-derived exact cohorts for core ratios and absolute capital/RWA values.
Strict cohorts match fiscal year and reporting basis, absolute cohorts also
match the explicit source unit, and special numeric values are excluded.
Median, quartiles/IQR, MAD, percentile rank, robust z-score, cohort size, and
coverage statuses are emitted with deterministic one-observation-per-FRN
selection. N<8 is suppressed, N=8–11 is labelled small, and MAD starts at
N>=12. Broad mode is coverage-only and cannot silently become a benchmark.

Added fixed-panel year-to-year rank movement using the same FRNs and basis in
both years, plus parent-group and peer-cluster benchmark guardrails. Group
benchmarks are suppressed below N=8; the current refresh therefore suppresses
all parent-group benchmarks and retains the two peer clusters as eligible
populations. Conventional confidence intervals are deliberately not shown
because the disclosed bank cohort is not a probability sample.

Integrated accessible, collapsible HTML sections and concise PDF summaries for
ratio distributions, absolute-value distributions, fixed-panel rank movement,
and group-model guardrails. Added regression coverage for explicit-basis
selection and refreshed the complete 145-bank HTML/PDF deliverables.

Resolution: IN-016 completion criteria met. Future threshold context and
interactive semantic-model work belongs in IN-018–IN-020.
