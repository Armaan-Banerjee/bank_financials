## Question

Can regulatory context be shown next to ratios without implying a universal
or timeless pass/fail conclusion?

## Scope

- Research and record dated, scoped thresholds/targets for CET1, Tier 1,
  total capital, leverage, liquidity, and MREL where authoritative sources
  support them.
- Model jurisdiction, firm scope, consolidation basis, effective dates,
  buffers/requirements versus management targets, and source citations.
- Show distance-to-context only when basis and scope match; otherwise show
  “context unavailable” with the reason.

## Completion criteria

- No threshold is hardcoded into an analysis or visual without source/date/scope.
- The model distinguishes regulatory minimum, buffer, target, and observed
  value.
- Historical observations use the benchmark version effective for that period,
  or explicitly state that historical matching is unavailable.

## Progress

Claimed 2026-08-30. Authoritative context research is recorded in
`research/regulatory_context_2026-08-30.md`, covering dated UK Pillar 1
capital minima, the capital conservation buffer, the scoped leverage
requirement/expectation, LCR and NSFR reference floors, and firm-specific
MREL. The implementation emits context records separately from observed
values and refuses to calculate distance when the observation basis, unit,
scope, or effective period cannot be matched.

`observation_context.csv` gives each annual observation an auditable
matched/unavailable status and reason.

The full refresh generated 8 versioned context records and 12,846 observation
context rows. No live distances are currently emitted because the database's
annual observations do not yet have classified reporting-basis values; this is
the intended safe outcome under the matching policy.

Resolution: IN-020 completion criteria met. Future basis classification or
firm-specific MREL loading can enable additional matched context without
changing the guardrail contract.
