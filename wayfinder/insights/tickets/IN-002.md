## Question

Build a mapping from each of the 145 built banks to its parent regulatory/
corporate group, where one exists, so IN-004 can compare entities within the
same group (e.g. does HSBC Bank plc move together with HSBC UK Bank Plc and
HSBC Innovation Bank Limited, or diverge?). This mapping does not currently
exist anywhere in the repo — `Banks List 2608.xlsx` is a flat list with no
parent-company column (confirmed by inspection while charting this map), so
this is real research, not a lookup.

This ticket is independent of IN-001 — it can be worked in parallel by a
different session/model with no shared-state conflict.

### What to produce

A durable parent-group graph and mapping file (e.g.
`research/bank_parent_groups.md`, with an edge-list section suitable for CSV
conversion) listing, for each of the 145 built banks:
- Its parent group name, if it has one (e.g. "HSBC Group", "Lloyds Banking
  Group", "NatWest Group") — many banks in this list are genuinely
  standalone/independent and should be recorded as such, not left blank
  ambiguously.
- The basis for the claim (Companies House ultimate-parent filing, FCA
  Financial Services Register, the bank's own annual report ownership
  disclosure, etc.) — a citation, same standard as the workbooks themselves.
- A flag for cases already known from prior build work to be tricky: entities
  that are legally distinct but share a public brand (e.g. Barclays Bank Plc
  vs. Barclays Bank UK PLC — confirmed as genuinely separate entities during
  WF-005, see `../../map.md`'s Decisions so far), or entities whose group
  changed mid-window (e.g. Kuwait Finance House's 2024 conversion from Ahli
  United Bank UK, noted in WF-015).

The mapping must preserve relationship hierarchy rather than flattening it:

- **Nodes:** each built UK bank/legal entity plus relevant intermediate and
  ultimate parent entities.
- **Edges:** directed, typed relationships such as `owned_by`,
  `ultimate_parent_of`, or `regulatory_consolidated_into`, with source citation
  and effective-date information where known.
- **Views:** a bank-level lookup table for IN-004, an edge list for analysis,
  and a Mermaid graph (at least for every multi-bank parent cluster) for human
  inspection. Immediate and ultimate parents should both be queryable.
- Legal ownership and regulatory/reporting consolidation must remain separate
  edge types; one must not be inferred from the other.

### Approach

Prioritize the FCA Financial Services Register and Companies House
group-structure/ultimate-parent filings as primary sources — both are
authoritative for UK-regulated entity ownership. Cross-check against each
bank's own annual report where the register is ambiguous (a bank often states
its own parent explicitly in the Directors' Report or Group Structure note).
Do not guess from bank names alone (e.g. "National Bank of X" does not
reliably imply a group relationship) — verify before recording.

Known clusters worth checking first, since they'll produce the richest
within-group comparisons for IN-004: HSBC (HSBC Bank plc, HSBC UK Bank Plc,
HSBC Innovation Bank Limited), Lloyds (Lloyds Bank plc, Lloyds Bank Corporate
Markets plc, Bank of Scotland, Halifax-related entities if present in the 145),
NatWest (NatWest Markets plc, National Westminster Bank plc, The Royal Bank of
Scotland plc, Ulster Bank-related entities if present), and any others that
surface during research (e.g. J.P. Morgan's multiple UK entities, Credit
Suisse's two UK entities noted as separately-reporting post-UBS-rescue in
WF-008).

## Progress

- Confirmed that `banks/` now contains all 145 built workbooks, including the
  five banks completed by WF-015.
- Began the parent-group evidence inventory from the existing builder entity
  notes and research files. No mapping output has been published yet; the
  next step is to resolve each bank to a cited parent group, confirmed
  standalone status, or documented ambiguity using primary sources.
- Mapping design decision confirmed: represent entities and parents as nodes
  with typed, cited, optionally time-bounded edges; provide both an edge list
  and Mermaid cluster views alongside the bank-level lookup table.

- Completed 2026-08-29: published `research/bank_parent_groups.md` covering
  all 145 workbook banks. It contains 145 lookup rows, typed ownership and
  regulatory-consolidation edges, effective-date notes for historical changes,
  and Mermaid views for the principal multi-bank clusters.
- Initial resolution summary: 119 rows had a confirmed group/parent, 19 were
  confirmed standalone, joint, consortium, or other non-single-parent cases,
  and 7 were initially provisional. No parent was inferred solely from a bank
  name, and legal ownership was kept separate from regulatory/reporting
  consolidation.
- Follow-up completed: primary-source checks resolved the seven provisional
  cases. Alpha Bank London, Birmingham Bank, and ClearBank are confirmed
  group-owned (with dates where ownership changed); Allica, Griffin, Monument,
  and GB Bank are confirmed standalone/non-single-parent ownership cases. The
  mapping now has 122 confirmed group/ownership rows, 23 confirmed
  standalone/joint/consortium/non-single-parent rows, and no provisional rows.
- Added an FRN column to all 145 bank lookup rows, using the validated
  `bank_metrics.csv` FRN assignments and preserving readable display names;
  all 145 mapping FRNs are unique and join exactly to the 145 metric FRNs.

## Resolution

`research/bank_parent_groups.md` is the durable graph/mapping deliverable for
IN-004. The confirmed clusters are ready for group comparisons; non-single-parent
rows must remain labelled with their ownership caveat in downstream analysis. Primary-source
URLs are retained in the bank builder source notes and the cited official
reports/registers used for each row.
