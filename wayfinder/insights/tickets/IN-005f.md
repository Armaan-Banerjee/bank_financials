## Scope

Replace the single global trend-period selector with one metric selector per
visible trend row and shared start/end fiscal-year inputs. Support arbitrary
available ranges, including FY2022→FY2025, and add total RWA alongside the
Pillar 3 ratios and operating cash flow.

## Progress

Implemented in the throwaway prototype. Trend counts are computed client-side
from the normalized FRN-keyed series rather than from hardcoded adjacent-year
summary rows. RWA uses total-RWA rows and excludes risk-component subtotals;
MREL remains present with sparse coverage.
