## Question

Can the subset of workbooks containing `Interim Pillar 3` disclosures be
added without contaminating annual comparisons?

## Scope

- Inventory interim sheets and cadence using `Extra Pillar 3 Disclosures.md`.
- Add explicit period type, period start/end or duration where extractable,
  publication/source metadata, and annual-vs-interim eligibility.
- Preserve raw labels and notes; never coerce a quarter or 15/18-month period
  into a normal annual observation.
- Add latest-snapshot, year-to-date, and annual-only query policies.

## Completion criteria

- Interim observations are in SQLite with a tested period policy.
- Annual trend results remain byte/reconciliation compatible.
- Coverage and cadence are visible in the HTML and query layer.
- Sparse/intermittent disclosure is explicitly labelled.

## Progress

Claimed on 2026-08-30. The extractor now has a standalone parser for the 14
real `Interim Pillar 3` sheets, preserving metric, unit, basis, raw period
label, value, and source-register boundaries. The period normalizer classifies
annual, quarterly, semi-annual, and point-in-time labels, including ISO dates
and reversed labels such as `H1 2025`; interim labels are not annual-eligible.

The live database remains at 12,846 annual rows. The interim rows are
intentionally not written to `annual_metrics`; they are now loaded into
separate `interim_observations` and `interim_source_register` tables. The
remaining work is to expose latest-snapshot/YTD policies and add deliverable
cadence/coverage summaries.

Validation on 2026-08-30: all 145 workbooks opened and extracted successfully;
all 145 matched an FRN; no expected annual sheets were missing; no workbook
produced zero output; and the raw annual extraction count was 12,881 before
the expected 35 exact-duplicate collapses. The live database and generated CSV
both contain 12,846 annual rows.

An early implementation was caught attempting to mix source-register rows into
annual metrics. It was removed before completion, the annual count restored,
and the clean annual refresh passed. The current live refresh writes 1,498
interim observations and 210 source-register rows without changing annual
outputs. Query-layer coverage and separation tests pass, and the HTML/PDF now
include the interim disclosure coverage summary.

## Resolution

Closed on 2026-08-30. Interim disclosures are stored separately from annual
metrics, with latest-snapshot and duration-aware query policies available.
Annual analyses remain isolated. Validation: 161 tests pass, the full refresh
succeeds, all 145 workbooks still open/extract/match correctly, and generated
HTML/PDF outputs contain the interim coverage section.
