## Question

Does a clickable parent-group view make entity-level alignment and divergence
more useful than a summary table alone?

## Scope

- Make each multi-entity parent group selectable.
- Show its built legal entities and latest comparable CET1/leverage values.
- Make missing data and reporting-basis/acquisition caveats explicit.
- Preserve the distinction between group membership and metric consolidation
  basis.

Keep the interaction inside the throwaway prototype. Do not redesign the IN-002
parent graph or create production database queries.

## Deliverable

Interactive parent-group drill-down with one aligned-group and one divergent-
group manual check. No bank workbooks or source datasets should change.
