## Question

What does each metric's distribution look like, and how much of it is
actually comparable?

## Scope

- Promote distributional outputs into HTML/PDF box/strip or violin views,
  percentile bands, and a bank-metric-year coverage heatmap.
- Use median, IQR/MAD, p10/p90, outlier flags, and comparable N; do not use
  arithmetic means as the headline statistic.
- Use broad comparability by default with an explicit strict-comparability
  view.
- Use log or robust scales for extreme positive amounts/ratios.
- Distinguish numeric, missing, non-numeric, shortened-period, and unknown-
  basis states.

## Completion criteria

- Visuals consume validated IN-016/IN-017-derived data with no hardcoded
  figures.
- Every chart states cohort, denominator, N, unit, and basis policy.
- HTML supports filtering and an accessible table; PDF works without hover.
- Tests cover skew, outliers, missingness, and broad/strict differences; full
  tests and refresh remain green.

## Resolution

Implemented 2026-08-30. `in023_distribution_visuals.py` now produces a
database-backed payload with p10/median/p90, IQR/MAD, explicit broad/strict
context, and bank × metric × year coverage states. The HTML deliverable adds
an accessible distribution-band view, filterable coverage table, and readable
legend; the PDF adds a no-hover distribution fallback. Added 6 focused tests,
updated the refresh order/documentation, regenerated the JSON/HTML/PDF outputs,
and verified the complete 203-test suite plus full refresh.
