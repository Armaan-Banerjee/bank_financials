## Scope

Allow the parent-group drill-down to choose which available metrics to display,
including capital, leverage, liquidity, and MREL where disclosed. Keep missing
values and sparse MREL coverage explicit.

## Progress

Implemented and validated in the prototype generator. Parent-group drill-downs
now offer CET1, Tier 1, Total Capital, Leverage, LCR, NSFR, and MREL selectors.
Imputed cluster values are suppressed and displayed as “Not available,” which
keeps sparse MREL coverage from being presented as disclosed data.
