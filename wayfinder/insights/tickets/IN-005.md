"prototype" per the wayfinder skill's Ticket Types.

### What to include

- Visual representation of IN-003's clusters (e.g. a scatter/dimensionality-
  reduced plot of banks colored by cluster, or a cluster-summary chart of
  each group's defining characteristics).

  charts, not just prose restating the document.
- Enough narrative framing that the charts are legible standalone to someone
  who hasn't read `Cross-Bank Trends Analysis.md` — this is a client
  deliverable, not an internal working doc.
- Rough is fine — the goal is a decision-enabling artifact, not a finished
  polished product. Don't over-invest in polish before the format itself is
  confirmed.
