# Extra Pillar 3 disclosures

Research date: 2026-08-29

This document identifies banks in the two Build Effort Review documents and
WF-013–WF-018 that have publicly evidenced Pillar 3 or explicitly equivalent
regulatory disclosures, grouped by observed publication cadence. It is a
research index, not a claim that every bank has published continuously in every
year.

Cadence is classified from official archives, disclosure policies, or multiple
dated first-party reports. “Annual / irregular” means that annual evidence
exists but the series has gaps, comparative-only years, or publication appears
to have stopped. Parent-group publications are not treated as entity-level
evidence without an explicit scope statement.

## Evidence that should not be promoted to a cadence group

The following were researched but should not be represented as confirmed
standalone Pillar 3 publication schedules without new primary evidence:

- J.P. Morgan Europe Limited and J.P. Morgan Securities plc (WF-014).
- KEXIM Bank (UK) Limited and Methodist Chapel Aid Limited (WF-015/WF-016).
- Nomura Bank International plc and Northern Bank Limited (WF-017/WF-018).
- ICBC (London) plc was included above only because later annual reports and
  comparative evidence were found; the same caution applies if an entity-level
  scope changes.
- Rathbones Investment Management Limited, where only wider Group Pillar 3
  material was found and it cannot substitute for the entity.
- Redwood Bank Limited, Santander Financial Services plc, Perenna Bank plc,
  Standard Chartered Bank, EFG Private Bank Limited, Close Brothers Limited,
  The Bank of London Group Limited, and other banks explicitly documented as
  having no standalone Pillar 3 series.

## Practical collection priority

For future non-annual collection, start with the quarterly/mixed group (HSBC
UK, Lloyds Bank, Lloyds Bank Corporate Markets, NatWest Markets, RBC Europe and
SMBC), then the semi-annual group (Aldermore, Co-operative Bank, Investec,
Metro, Paragon and Secure Trust). Keep each bank’s legal-entity and
parent-group basis in the collection key; a parent’s quarterly report must not
be silently used for a subsidiary that publishes only annually or not at all.
