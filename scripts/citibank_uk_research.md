# Citibank UK Limited (WF-025) research note

## Identity and basis

Citibank UK Limited (CUKL) is Companies House company **11283101** and FRN **124579**. The official 2023 Pillar 3 report says the disclosures are prepared on a stand-alone basis and that CUKL has no subsidiaries. This scope is therefore CUKL, not Citibank N.A. London Branch or Citibank Europe plc.

## Official source coverage

- [Companies House filing history](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/11283101/filing-history): 2024 full accounts filed 30 May 2025; 2025 accounts not filed as at 29 August 2026.
- [CUKL Pillar 3 Disclosures December 2021](https://www.citigroup.com/rcs/citigpa/akpublic/storage/public/b3p3d211231_uk.pdf): standalone 2021 KM1 and liquidity/leverage tables.
- [CUKL Pillar 3 Disclosures December 2023](https://www.citigroup.com/rcs/citigpa/storage/public/b3p3d231231_uk.pdf): standalone 2023 KM1 with 2022 comparatives, including capital, RWA, leverage, LCR and NSFR.
- [CUKL Annual Financial Statements 2024](https://www.citigroup.com/rcs/citigpa/storage/public/citibank-uk-limited-annual-fs-2024.pdf): FRS 101 IAS 7 cash-flow exemption (note 1, p. 25) and reference to CUKL Basel Pillar 3 disclosures.
- [Citi Other Regulatory Filings](https://www.citigroup.com/global/investors/other-regulatory-filings): official regulatory disclosure index.

## Build decision

Built a Pillar-3-only workbook for FY2021-FY2023. FY2022 is the comparative column in the official 2023 report, not an extrapolation. FY2024 is omitted because an official standalone CUKL Pillar 3 document was not located during this pass; FY2025 is omitted because the statutory accounts are not yet filed and no official standalone Pillar 3 report was located. NSFR is blank for FY2021 because the 2021 disclosure does not report it and explains that NSFR was to be implemented from 2022. MREL is retained as a required sheet but marked not publicly disclosed; the reports state that no MREL-eligible debt had been issued.
