# Afin Bank Limited — WF revisit research

## Conclusion

A defensible short-period workbook can be built for Afin Bank Limited using FY2024 only. The bank's first official
Pillar 3 disclosure is for 31 December 2024 and explicitly says that no prior-period comparatives are provided. The
workbook therefore does not manufacture FY2021–FY2023 or FY2025 values.

## Entity and reporting basis

- Afin Bank Limited is company 13090556 and FRN 1004742. The Companies House overview identifies the company as an
  active UK bank and shows its last accounts made up to 31 December 2024: <https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/13090556>
- The company was formerly All Africa Capital Limited. Its 2024 annual report says that it received banking
  authorisation with restrictions in October 2024 and subsequently changed its name: <https://afinbank.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Afin-Bank-Ltd-Year-End-Accounts-FV24-FINAL-SIGNED.pdf>
- The Pillar 3 disclosure states that the scope applies to Afin Bank Limited only and that the company is a
  majority-owned subsidiary of WAICA Reinsurance Corporation PLC. Parent-level figures are not substituted:
  <https://afinbank.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Afin-Bank-2024-Pillar-3-Disclosures-FINAL.pdf>

The source explains that Afin had no qualifying cash outflows and therefore reports LCR as `999999%`. The workbook
reproduces that value as reported and does not reinterpret it as a conventional finite ratio. No numeric MREL ratio
was found in the 2024 Pillar 3 report or audited accounts, so the MREL sheet is explicitly marked not publicly
disclosed.

## Cash-flow coverage

The audited 2024 annual report contains a standalone statement of cash flows on printed page 36. The workbook uses
the FY2024 figures in pounds, without converting them to thousands:

- Net cash outflow from operating activities: £5,854,403
- Net cash used in investing activities: £12,345,649
- Net cash generated from financing activities: £20,384,295
- Net increase in cash and cash equivalents: £2,184,243
- Cash and cash equivalents at beginning of year: £971,678
- Cash and cash equivalents at end of year: £3,155,921

The statement reconciles arithmetically: operating plus investing plus financing equals the reported net increase,
and opening cash plus the net increase equals closing cash.

Source: Afin Bank Limited, *Annual Report and Financial Statements for the year ended 31 December 2024*, printed p. 36,
<https://afinbank.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Afin-Bank-Ltd-Year-End-Accounts-FV24-FINAL-SIGNED.pdf>. The 2024 accounts
were filed at Companies House on 29 September 2025; see the filing history and filed document:
<https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/13090556/filing-history> and
<https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/13090556/filing-history/MzQ4MjgwOTc2M2FkaXF6a2N4/document?download=0&format=pdf>.

## Build decision

Build a standard 13-sheet workbook with one FY2024 column: Overview, Cash Flow Statement, the 11 standard Pillar 3
metric sheets. This is a short-period exception, not a completed five-year history. Revisit after FY2025 accounts and
Pillar 3 disclosures are published if a longer time series is required.
