Net carrying value (Gross carrying value less Loss allowance) does not tie exactly to the Balance Sheet's 'Loans and advances to customers - at amortised cost' line (e.g. FY2025: £4,528,886k here vs £4,540,376k on the Balance Sheet) - Note 30's 'financial assets at amortised cost' scope is evidently broader than customer loans alone (likely also captures loans to banks and/or investment securities held at amortised cost), not confirmed further from the disclosure alone - not force-reconciled.
