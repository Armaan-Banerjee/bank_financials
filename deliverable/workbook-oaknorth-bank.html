No Basel II-era terminology gap arises anywhere in FY2015-FY2020: OakNorth's entire trading history postdates the EU Capital Requirements Directive IV (CRD IV, in force from 1 January 2014), so the CET1/Tier 1/Total Capital vocabulary used throughout this workbook was already the Bank's own from its first Pillar 3 disclosure (year ended 31 December 2015).
