WHY FY2021, FY2020 AND FY2019 ARE BLANK - the sharpest trap in this bank, and the reason a title search alone is not a presence test. The FY2020 and FY2021 editions DO print a table titled 'Table 1: Key metrics (KM1/IFRS9-FL)' - and it is NOT this template. What they actually print is the EBA-era IFRS9-FL layout, numbered 1-17 in one continuous run: 1 CET1 capital, 2 CET1 capital as-if, 3 Tier 1 capital, 4 Tier 1 capital as-if, 5 Total regulatory capital, 6 Total capital as-if, 7 Total RWAs, 8 Total RWAs as-if, 9-14 the six capital ratios (transitional and as-if), 15 total leverage ratio exposure measure, 16 leverage ratio, 17 leverage ratio as-if - with an UNNUMBERED four-row buffer block ('Capital conservation buffer requirement', 'Countercyclical buffer requirement', 'Other Systemically Important Institutions (O-SII) buffer requirement', 'Total of bank CET1 specific buffer requirements') and a two-row capital-requirement block ('Total capital requirement (Pillar 1 + Pillar 2A)', 'CET1 available after meeting the bank's minimum capital requirements') spliced in between rows 14 and 15. It differs from the UK KM1 in every way that matters:
* its own asterisk footnote says the references 'identify the lines prescribed in the European Banking Authority (EBA) templates', where the FY2022 edition onward says 'the relevant PRA template';
* it has NO LCR rows and NO NSFR rows whatsoever - the entire second half of KM1;
* it has no UK-7a/UK-7b/UK-7c SREP split;
* and its row numbers denote different things. Its row 2 is 'CET1 capital as if IFRS 9 transitional arrangements had not been applied'; KM1's row 2 is 'Tier 1 capital'. Its row 7 is 'Total RWAs'; KM1's row 7 is the total capital ratio.
Transcribing those figures into this sheet's rows would assert a row-for-row correspondence HSBC UK never published, so FY2019-FY2021 are left blank instead. This cuts the opposite way from the usual trap: a bank can print the token 'KM1' in a table title without printing the template, just as another bank (Allica) can print the template without ever writing 'KM1'. Nothing is lost - those editions' capital, RWA, ratio and leverage figures are carried on the single-metric sheets in this workbook, sourced from those tables directly. FY2019's edition is a step further back again: its Table 1 is titled 'Comparison of own funds, capital and leverage ratios, with and without the application of transitional arrangements for IFRS 9' and has no buffer block at all. The positive reason for the change is in the FY2022 edition's own footnote 2: the SREP, buffer, leverage and liquidity disclosures 'have been implemented from 1 January 2022, and are based on the PRA's disclosure templates and instructions which came into force at that time'.
