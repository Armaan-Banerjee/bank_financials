Its own FY2021 Pillar 3 (p.19, Table LR Sum) carries the line 'Less: central bank exposures (160,863)' for 2021 and '(125,844)' for 2020, footnoted 'Exposures to central banks subject to a maximum of deposits in the same currency is excluded from the leverage ratio exposure measure', and cites PS21/17 'UK leverage ratio: treatment of claims on central banks (October 2017)'.
