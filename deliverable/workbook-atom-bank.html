ENTITY NOTE: Atom Bank Plc (company 08632552, FRN 661960, matches Banks List 2608.xlsx exactly) is the PRA-authorised entity built here. During FY2023, a new non-trading holding company - Atom Holdco Limited (later re-registered Atom Holdco plc ahead of its 2025 IPO-track listing) - was inserted above Atom Bank Plc in the group structure. From that point, Atom Bank Plc became a wholly-owned subsidiary whose results are consolidated into Atom Holdco's own published financial statements, and Pillar 3 disclosures moved from being published at Bank level to being published at Holdco (Group) level, with a separate Bank-solo column shown alongside Group in the KM1 template from FY2023 onward. All Pillar 3 figures in this workbook use the Bank column (or, for FY2021-FY2022, the single pre-restructuring figure that IS the Bank, per each report's own note: 'the comparatives reflect the previous structure where Atom bank plc was the sole regulated entity') - so there is no cash-flow-vs-Pillar-3 basis mismatch here, unlike several other banks in this project.
