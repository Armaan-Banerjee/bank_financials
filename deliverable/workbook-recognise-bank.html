No note discloses the exact year this reclassification took effect (no debt securities were held at all in FY2022-FY2024); the FY2025 balance in Note 17 is disclosed only under the amortised-cost heading, so the same balance is shown there. Because the entire balance is one issuer/bucket in every year, the row is labelled by issuer type only, not renamed to a 'Total ...' line (no sub-rows apply).
