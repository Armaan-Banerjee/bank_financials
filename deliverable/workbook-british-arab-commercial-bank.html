- A smaller, similar GBP42k reclass between Retained earnings and Fair value reserve exists between FY2021's own closing balance and FY2022's own opening balance (Total equity unaffected); shown the same way.
- FY2021's own report's Net operating income (GBP53,466k) and Other operating income (GBP1,519k) differ immaterially (GBP21k) from the FY2022 report's own FY2021 comparative (GBP53,487k / GBP1,540k); FY2021's own figures are used here.
