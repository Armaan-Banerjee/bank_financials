The Group includes Oxbury Bank Plc and its wholly owned subsidiary Oxbury Earth Ltd (and, from FY2025, Oxbury Earth LLC); Group figures are not substituted for the Bank's own figures. The FY2025 report describes a new wider holding structure, but the legal bank entity remains Oxbury Bank Plc.

HISTORY NOTE: the company was incorporated 25 May 2018 as 'Oxbury FS Plc', a pre-launch shell building its technology platform - it renamed to Oxbury Bank Plc in mid-2020 after being authorised by the PRA/FCA as a credit institution in January 2020, then spent all of FY2020 in a regulatory 'mobilisation' phase with no lending or deposit-taking, finally launching to customers on 29 January 2021. FY2018-FY2020 are therefore genuine Oxbury financial statements (single-entity, no Group/Company split existed yet - there was no subsidiary) but reflect a pre-trading company, not a functioning bank: nil interest income, nil loans, nil customer deposits throughout. FY2018 covers a short first period (25 May 2018 - 31 Dec 2018), not a full year.
