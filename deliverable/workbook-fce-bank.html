WHY verify_workbook.py REPORTS BLOCK MISMATCHES ON THE P&L AND CASH FLOW SHEETS (checked 2026-09-16, all benign). That checker sums only the DATA rows since the last TOTAL row, which is the right rule for a flat statement and the wrong one for FCE's, because FCE's P&L nests subtotals (Net interest income and Net fees and commissions income both feed Total income; Total income and Operating expenses both feed Profit before tax). Every flagged line reconciles once the skipped subtotal is included - e.g. FY2025 Total income 390+32+0=422, Profit before tax 422-31-265-15-31+87=167, Profit after tax 167-71=96, and FY2024 Profit for the period 55+157=212 and Total comprehensive income 212-42-27-134=9. On the Cash Flow sheet the same thing happens at the closing-cash tail, where the checker omits the 'Net (decrease)/increase' TOTAL row: FY2025 -162+1253+20=1111 and FY2024 -381+2557-50-873=1253, both exactly the printed closing balance. Note also that 'Operating expenses' is a single line item that happens to be bolded, not a subtotal of the ECL row above it. No figure on either sheet is in question.

WHY verify_workbook.py REPORTS BLOCK MISMATCHES ON THE P&L AND CASH FLOW SHEETS (checked 2026-09-16, all benign). That checker sums only the DATA rows since the last TOTAL row, which is the right rule for a flat statement and the wrong one for FCE's, because FCE's P&L nests subtotals (Net interest income and Net fees and commissions income both feed Total income; Total income and Operating expenses both feed Profit before tax). Every flagged line reconciles once the skipped subtotal is included - e.g. FY2025 Total income 390+32+0=422, Profit before tax 422-31-265-15-31+87=167, Profit after tax 167-71=96, and FY2024 Profit for the period 55+157=212 and Total comprehensive income 212-42-27-134=9. On the Cash Flow sheet the same thing happens at the closing-cash tail, where the checker omits the 'Net (decrease)/increase' TOTAL row: FY2025 -162+1253+20=1111 and FY2024 -381+2557-50-873=1253, both exactly the printed closing balance. Note also that 'Operating expenses' is a single line item that happens to be bolded, not a subtotal of the ECL row above it. No figure on either sheet is in question.
