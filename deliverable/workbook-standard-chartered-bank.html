WHAT THE FY2021 EDITION ACTUALLY CONTAINS, AND WHY IT CANNOT FILL THESE CELLS. Its Annex 1 'Standard Chartered Significant Subsidiaries' does carry a Standard Chartered Bank column (Table 99 capital resources, Tables 100-108, Table 109 overview of RWA, Table 110 leverage), with a 2020 comparative. But that column is the SCB GROUP CONSOLIDATED basis, not solo consolidation: the narrative above Table 100 states 'Tables 100 to 108 summarise the CONSOLIDATED capital position of Standard Chartered Bank', and footnote 1 to Table 99 states that the column 'aligns with the capital section of the 2021 Standard Chartered Bank Accounts'. Its FY2021 total RWA is 193,988 $m and FY2020 187,901 $m, against 142,161 $m for FY2021 on the solo-consolidated basis carried elsewhere on this sheet - three different perimeters, not three measurements of one. Importing it would mix bases inside a single row, which this workbook does not do. The FY2020, FY2019 and FY2018 editions have no significant-subsidiaries capital annex at all.
