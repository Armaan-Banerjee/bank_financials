Guaranty Trust Bank (UK) Limited (FRN 466611, Companies House 05969821) is a wholly-owned UK
subsidiary of Guaranty Trust Bank Limited ('GTBank Nigeria'), itself wholly owned by Guaranty Trust
Holding Company Plc (GTCO). Unlike several other Nigerian-bank-owned UK subsidiaries in this project
(Zenith Bank UK, UBA UK, FCMB UK, FidBank UK), this entity reports in GBP throughout - no FX
conversion is required. FY2025 accounts (year ended 31 Dec 2025) had not yet been filed with
Companies House as of this build (last filing: FY2024, filed 6 May 2025); FY2025 is left blank.

DATA QUALITY NOTE - cash-equivalents basis discontinuity: the FY2022 Annual Report states that
'cash and cash equivalents for 2021 have been re-presented to include money market placements with
maturity of less than 3 months', quantifying the impact as an increase in the FY2021 opening balance
from GBP193,625,102 to GBP276,931,087 and in the FY2021 closing balance from GBP174,352,252 to
GBP290,627,822. However, neither of those 'old-basis' figures matches FY2021's own originally-filed
Annual Report, which itself shows an opening balance of GBP83,305,985 and a closing balance of
GBP116,275,570 for FY2021 - a discrepancy in the source documents themselves that could not be
traced or resolved. Per this project's convention, each year below uses its own originally-published
figures (so FY2021's closing balance will not visually chain into FY2022's opening balance); this gap
is flagged here rather than forced to reconcile, the same treatment as Arab Bank Europe's unexplained
cash-bridge gap elsewhere in this project.

Also note: FY2021's own 'Operating cash flows before movement in working capital' subtotal
(GBP(10,764,152)) is GBP360 different from the sum of its own printed component lines - an immaterial
source rounding artifact, not corrected.
