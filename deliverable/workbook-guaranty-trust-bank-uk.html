and-advances-to-BANKS ECL alone. The scope of the FY2025 figure is therefore unresolved on the face of the
document, and it is not attributable to any single row of that sheet. It is recorded here instead of being
guessed into place.

Three candidate explanations were tested and all three were ruled out. (1) ROW MISALIGNMENT - ruled out by rendering each edition's page at 300 dpi and reading the ruled table cells directly rather than trusting `pdftotext`. That mattered: in the FY2025 edition the text layer emits the 2025 column of several rows on its own line ABOVE the row label (an artefact of vertical centring in taller cells), which is exactly what a column shift would look like. same treatment already applied to FY2024 (37,000,000 + 11,019,326 + 56,053 = 48,075,379).

Tier 1/Total Capital sheets therefore show FY2025 as 54,604,000 - the disclosed thousands figure
scaled, not an exact-to-the-pound amount. Earlier years remain exact to the pound.
